Days, Parts of the Day & Time

Learn to talk about schedules, days of the week, and tell the time using o’clock, half past, quarter past, and quarter to.

7Days
4Parts of day
4Time expressions
A1Schedules

Learning Objectives

  • 📅Name the days of the week and parts of the day (morning, afternoon, evening, night).
  • ⏰Tell the time using o’clock, half past, quarter past, quarter to.
  • 🗣️Talk about schedules: Today is Monday. I study in the afternoon.
  • 👂Listen and draw/write times (digital to phrases).
  • 🔊Pronounce time expressions with natural rhythm and stress.

Why This Matters

Telling the time and knowing days / parts of the day is essential for making plans, understanding schedules, and talking about your daily life. You’ll use these expressions in conversations about work, school, and free time.

⚠ Capitalization rule
In English, days of the week always start with a capital letter: Monday, Tuesday, etc. Parts of the day (morning, afternoon, evening, night) are not capitalized unless they start a sentence.

Telling the Time & Prepositions

How to say the time and use in, on, at with days and parts of the day.

🕒 Time expressions
ExpressionMeaningExample
o’clocken puntoIt’s three o’clock.
half pasty mediaIt’s half past four.
quarter pasty cuartoIt’s quarter past two.
quarter tomenos cuartoIt’s quarter to ten.
📅 Prepositions: in / on / at
PrepositionUseExample
athora exactaI wake up at 7 o’clock.
inparte del díaI study in the morning.
ondías de la semanaWe play soccer on Saturday.
📝 Structure examples
Today is + day → Today is Monday.
It is + time → It is half past seven.
Subject + verb + in the + part of day → I study in the afternoon.
On + day → She works on Friday.

Dialogue 2 — Weekly schedule

Dialogue · When do you study?
A
"When do you study English?"
B
"I study in the morning on Mondays and Wednesdays."
A
"What about Saturday?"
B
"On Saturday I play soccer at half past nine in the morning."
💡 Key phrases
  • →What time is it? — It’s half past seven.
  • →What day is today? — Today is Tuesday.
  • →I study in the morning / afternoon / evening.
  • →On + day + I + verb + at + time.

Pronunciation: Time Rhythm

Stress and intonation in time expressions.

ˈhaːf pɑːst
Half past — stress on "half"
half past threehalf past nine
The main stress falls on the hour number: half past FOUR, half past TEN. The "half past" is said quickly.
ˈkwɔː.tə ˈpɑːst
Quarter past / quarter to
quarter past twoquarter to five
Same rhythm: stress on "quarter" and the hour. "Quarter to" has a slight pause? No, it links: quarter_to_five → /kwɔːtətəfaɪv/.
🗣️ Practice sentence
"I wake up at half past six in the morning, and I go to bed at quarter to eleven at night."
