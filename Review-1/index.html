⚠ Grammar Tip — Adjective Order
In English, adjectives come after the verb "to be" (She is tall) or before the noun (She is a tall girl).
✗ "She tall is." → ✓ "She is tall."

Topic 10 · Question Words

Question Words
What · Where · Who · When · How

Learn to ask and answer basic questions using the 5 WH-words. This is the key to starting real conversations.

The 5 Question Words

What
¿Qué?
"What is your name?"
Where
¿Dónde?
"Where do you live?"
Who
¿Quién?
"Who is she?"
When
¿Cuándo?
"When do you study?"
How
¿Cómo? / ¿Qué tal?
"How are you?" · "How old are you?"

Question Structures

With Verb "To Be"
What is your name? → My name is Ana.
Where are you from? → I am from Mexico.
Who is she? → She is my sister.
How are you? → I am fine.
With Action Verbs (Present Simple)
What do you do? → I am a student.
Where do you live? → I live in Lima.
When do you study? → I study in the morning.
How do you go to school? → I go by bus.
💡 Key Rule
WH-word + auxiliary + subject + verb?
What do you eat for breakfast?
